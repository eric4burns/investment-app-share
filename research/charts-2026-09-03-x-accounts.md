## What they have in common

1. **Buy the retest, sell into the extension.** Every equity chart is a level below price that held before, or a level above where the trim goes. Nobody here buys a breakout on the day.
2. **Fibonacci does two jobs.** Retracements (0.5 / 0.618 / 0.786 / 0.887) say where a pullback ends; extensions (1.272 / 1.414 / 1.618 / 1.886) say where the next leg goes. The deep 0.786–0.887 band appears on four charts as the buy zone.
3. **A level changes sides.** "Previous support is now resistance" (Con) and the retest-from-above of a broken shelf (AsafNaaman15 on ASST, the user's own TEM at 62) are the same rule in two directions.
4. **The record is the argument.** RonnieV and the AEVA chart both write what the signal did LAST time beside it. That is the app's own measurement rule, done by hand, per name.
5. **Structure on the oscillator.** Freedom By 40's RSI trendlines and the AEVA chart's RSI divergence line are the third and fourth people in this project's sources to draw on the lower pane.
6. **Wave counts are a frame, not a rule.** Three of the accounts count waves, but every actionable thing they draw is a Fibonacci zone or a trendline the app can compute. The count itself is not encoded.
7. **Volume by price is the one new tool.** SteveUrkeldude's histogram answers "how much traded here" instead of "when," and it is the piece the app did not have.

## How the items combine (2026-09-04, watchlist replay, weekly at 63 days)

`research/audits/measure-2026-09-04-pairs-W63.md`, from `measure.pairs`: for pairs that fire together on enough calls and dates, the residual of calls carrying both against the sum of what each does alone. The reading that matters for the question "how do these work together":

- **Bearish trend readings that agree are followed by outperformance beyond the sum.** Cloud below with lower highs and lows: +9.48 together against −4.58 additive (lift +14, t 6.4). Death cross with a Fibonacci ceiling: +10.52 against +6.86 (t 7.1). Death cross with lower highs and lows: +10.81 against +1.66. A name where everything looks broken on the weekly is the washed-out case, and the more readings agree, the more it has already fallen.
- **A confirmed bearish pattern with anything is followed by underperformance beyond the sum.** Double top confirmed with a bullish trendline: −14.72 against −5.67 (t −7.6); with bearish relative strength −18.12 against −4.51. The confirmed break is the one bear reading on the weekly that is not inverted, and it does not need company.
- **The level readings do not add to each other.** Reversal zone with on-balance volume: −11.34 against −1.37; role flip with a bearish trendline: −7.58 against −1.33. Two level readings agreeing is worse than either, which is the weekly inversion compounding.

None of this changes a weight. It says which items are redundant (two level readings), which confirm each other (bear trend items, in the inverted direction), and which stand alone (the confirmed pattern), and that is the shape any combined rule has to respect.
